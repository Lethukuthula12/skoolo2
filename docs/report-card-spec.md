# Report cards — what South African policy requires

Researched 2026-10-22 for roadmap Stage 3. Two Department of Basic Education documents govern
report cards for Grades R to 9:

- **National Policy pertaining to the Programme and Promotion Requirements of the National
  Curriculum Statement Grades R–12** (NPPPR), DBE copy dated 5 February 2021.
  <https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Policies/NATIONAL%20POLICY%20NPPPR%20%2005%20Feb%202021.pdf>
- **National Protocol for Assessment Grades R–12** (NPA).
  <https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Policies/NatProtAssess.pdf>

Paragraph numbers below refer to those documents. Independent schools follow the same
National Curriculum Statement, and the NPA's rule against withholding reports applies to them too.

## The scale of achievement

The same seven levels apply from Grade R to Grade 9 (NPPPR tables 4, 9 and 17):

| Code | Description | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Outstanding achievement | 80–100 |
| 6 | Meritorious achievement | 70–79 |
| 5 | Substantial achievement | 60–69 |
| 4 | Adequate achievement | 50–59 |
| 3 | Moderate achievement | 40–49 |
| 2 | Elementary achievement | 30–39 |
| 1 | Not achieved | 0–29 |

The percentage decides the code. Skoolo computes the code from the mark; nobody types it.

What appears on the report per phase (NPA 17(4)):

| Phase | Grades | Report shows |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | R–3 | Code and description. A percentage may be shown as well (NPA 25(8)(c): "a national code and/or a percentage"). The pilot school wants percentages. |
| Intermediate | 4–6 | Code, description and percentage. |
| Senior | 7–9 | Code, description and percentage. |

Each language is reported separately at its level, for example "Home Language: isiZulu" and
"First Additional Language: English" (NPA 17(6)).

## What every report card must contain (NPA 25(8))

| Component | Required content | Where Skoolo gets it |
|---|---|---|
| Personal details | Learner's name, grade and class, date of birth, attendance profile | Learner register, class (Stage 1); days absent per term (entered with the marks, later from Stage 4 absence notes) |
| Official school details | Year and term; school name; date; signature and comment of parent or guardian, teacher and principal; school closing and opening dates; school stamp; explanation of the national codes | School profile; term dates (new school setting); teacher and principal names from the staff register; principal approval recorded in Skoolo; stamp uploaded like the badge; the code table printed on every report |
| Performance details | A code and/or percentage per subject, and a description of strengths and developmental needs | Marks per subject; teacher comment per subject or overall |
| Constructive feedback | Comments on performance compared with previous performance | Teacher comment; Skoolo shows the previous term's mark next to the current one |

Other rules:

- Formal reports go to parents **once a term** (NPA 25(2)).
- The term's report shows that term's performance; the **end-of-year report shows cumulative
  performance for the year** (NPA 25(4)–(5)).
- **No errors, erasures or corrections**; the school management team is responsible (NPA 25(3)).
  In Skoolo: the principal approves before anything is published, a published report is locked,
  and a correction creates a new version with a record of who changed what.
- Reports may be produced electronically in the school's own style, provided they contain all of
  the above (NPA 25(9)).
- Parents have the right of access to their children's reports (NPA 25(12)).
- **Schools may not withhold report cards from learners for any reason whatsoever (NPA 25(13)).**
  When school fees arrive in Stage 5, arrears must never block a report.

## Subjects per grade (NPPPR chapters 3–5, CAPS time allocation)

Offered as defaults when a school sets up its subjects; the school edits them and may add its own,
such as Religious Studies or Bible Studies.

| Grades | Subjects |
|---|---|
| R | Home Language, Mathematics, Life Skills |
| 1–3 | Home Language, First Additional Language, Mathematics, Life Skills |
| 4–6 | Home Language, First Additional Language, Mathematics, Natural Sciences and Technology, Social Sciences, Life Skills |
| 7–9 | Home Language, First Additional Language, Mathematics, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences, Technology, Economic and Management Sciences, Life Orientation, Creative Arts |

Grades 00, 0 and RR are before Grade R and outside CAPS; the National Curriculum Framework for
children from birth to four applies. Their reports are usually descriptive. How the pilot school
reports on them is an open question in the roadmap.

Grades 10 to 12 are the Further Education and Training (FET) phase. The grade list already runs to
Grade 12, so a high school can set its range, create classes and open enrolment for these grades
today. Their reports are different, though: subjects are elective combinations rather than a fixed
list, promotion follows the National Senior Certificate rather than the Grade R–9 guidelines above,
and reporting is in percentages (national codes are not required past Grade 9). The FET subject
sets and promotion rules are not specified here and will be needed only when a high school actually
uses Skoolo for marks and reports (Stages 3 and 7). Classes, learners and enrolment for Grades 8–12
need nothing extra.

## Progression and promotion guidelines

Used for the end-of-year report and the pass and fail lists the school asked for.

| Grades | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R | Level 4 (50%) in Home Language and Level 3 (40%) in Mathematics (NPPPR 7(2)) |
| 1–3 | Level 4 in Home Language, Level 3 in First Additional Language, Level 3 in Mathematics (NPPPR 7(3)) |
| 4–6 | Level 4 in Home Language, Level 3 in First Additional Language, Level 3 in Mathematics, and Level 3 in any two other subjects (NPPPR 14(2)) |
| 7–9 | Of nine subjects, meet the requirements in eight, provided the school-based assessment of the ninth is complete: Level 4 in Home Language, Level 3 in First Additional Language, Level 3 in Mathematics, Level 3 in any three other subjects, and at least Level 2 in any two others (NPPPR 21(1)) |

Further rules:

- Progression within the age cohort is the norm in Grades R to 6 unless the learner cannot cope
  with the next grade (NPPPR 7(1), 14(1)).
- A learner may be retained **only once per phase**, so no one spends more than four years in a
  phase. A learner who would otherwise be retained a second time is **progressed** with support.
  The end-of-year outcome is therefore one of: promoted, progressed, or retained.
- There are concessions for Deaf learners (SASL at Home Language level) and for immigrant learners
  (exemption from one official language). Skoolo records the concession on the learner and
  applies the matching rule; it does not decide eligibility.
- Promotion marks: Grades R–3 are 100% school-based assessment; Grades 4–6 are 75% school-based
  assessment and 25% end-of-year examination (NPPPR 8(2), 15(2)). Grades 7–9 include an
  internally set end-of-year examination (NPPPR 22(2)). In Stage 3 the school enters each term's
  mark and the final mark per subject; the weighting is calculated by Skoolo only from Stage 7,
  when individual assessments are captured.

The guidelines inform the decision; the school makes it. Skoolo shows "meets the requirements" or
"does not meet the requirements" per learner, and the principal confirms the outcome.

## Schedules (NPA 26) — the class and grade export

A schedule is the per-grade summary completed four times a year, and it is what the school asked
for when it wanted class lists filtered by pass and fail. It contains:

- School name and stamp, date.
- Each learner's name and surname, admission number, date of birth, gender and age.
- Number of years in the phase (end-of-year schedule only).
- Code and percentage per subject.
- At the end of the year, for Grades R–8, whether each learner is ready to progress.
- Signatures of teacher, principal and departmental official (end-of-year only).

Skoolo exports it to Excel and PDF per class or per grade, filterable by outcome.

## What this means for Skoolo's data

- **Learner register (Stage 1):** add an admission number and the number of years in the current
  phase, alongside the fields the school already keeps (name, surname, date of birth, ID number,
  sex, grade, contact numbers, enrolment date).
- **School settings:** term dates (opening and closing) per year, school stamp image, principal.
- **Subjects:** per grade, with the language level (Home Language, First Additional Language)
  and the language name.
- **Term results:** per learner, subject and term: mark, computed code; per learner and term: days
  absent, teacher comment, principal approval, publication status and version.
- **Year results:** final mark per subject, outcome (promoted, progressed, retained) confirmed by
  the principal.
